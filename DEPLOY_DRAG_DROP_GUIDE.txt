1. Find the "Kids_Learning" folder

2. Drag the entire folder to the deploy zone in Netlify

TROUBLESHOOTING:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ Blank page after deployment?
✅ Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
✅ Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)

❌ Styles not loading?
✅ Wait 60 seconds for caching to clear
✅ Try in an Incognito/Private window

❌ Can't find the Deploys tab?
✅ Make sure you're on the right site:
   https://app.netlify.com/sites/speakbuddy-kids-learning

❌ Can't drag files?
✅ Try dragging the folder itself (not individual files)
✅ Make sure the folder contains index.html
