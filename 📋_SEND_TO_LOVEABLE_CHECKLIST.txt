🎯 KEY TALKING POINTS FOR LOVEABLE
================================================================================

1. SEAMLESS CONVERSATION LOOP
   The app's core magic: voice in → process → speak → auto-listen (repeat)
   No UI friction, no "send" button. Feels like texting, but with voice.

2. TOPIC-AWARE CONVERSATION ENGINE
   Innovation: Stay on SAME topic for 3–6 turns instead of jumping randomly.
   Makes conversations feel human-like, not robotic chatbot.

3. TWO AI ENGINES
   - Claude Sonnet 4.6 (when configured) for best quality
   - Local fallback (always available) for graceful degradation
   Never leaves user without AI.

4. INDIAN ENGLISH IS NON-NEGOTIABLE
   Female voice (Veena, Lekha, Priya, Aditi)
   Slow speech rate (0.80–0.84) — kids need time to follow
   Warm tone ("Wah, shabash!", "Ekdum nice!", "Arre, wonderful!")
   Topics: Cricket, Diwali, samosas, school exams, monsoons, families

5. BEAUTIFUL SESSION REPORTS
   Not just scores — personalized, encouraging, parent-friendly
   Priya reads summary aloud (reinforces learning)
   Specific praise points (not generic)

6. 4-LEVEL PROGRESSION SYSTEM
   Auto-adapts difficulty based on composite score
   Levels: Beginner → Basic Speaker → Intermediate → Advanced Communicator
   Kids feel achievement (not just grinded endlessly)

7. GAMIFICATION KEEPS KIDS COMING BACK
   50 badges across 9 categories
   Streaks (🔥 7-Day Streak!)
   Daily challenges (+50 XP)
   XP tiers (Starter → Bronze → Silver → Gold)
